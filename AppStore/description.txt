Delve — Fixed-Dungeon Puzzle Crawler

One handcrafted tomb. Zero randomness. Everything you learn, you keep.

Delve is an offline, single-player adventure game about a fixed dungeon that never reshuffles. Explore room by room with simple one-thumb controls, read a cryptic quest that points at goals without giving you the route, and figure out which puzzles must be solved first — because the sealed door down the hall only opens if you pulled the right lever three rooms ago.

Delve rewards observation, notes, and deliberate backtracking instead of grinding.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

• Fixed, handcrafted dungeon — every room, secret, and puzzle placement is designed, not generated. Your knowledge is the real progress.
• Order-of-operations puzzles — some goals dead-end the naive route. Experiment, backtrack, retry. Nothing you do can softlock your run.
• A genuinely vague quest — goals report only what your actions actually proved (unknown / in progress / achieved). No quest markers, no hand-holding.
• Clue journal — pin your own notes to rooms and track quest theories in a journal that stays entirely yours. Plan your next push at the entrance.
• Honest run records — your steps, sessions, and discoveries are derived from an append-only ledger of what actually happened.

BUILT FOR REAL LIFE

• 100% offline. No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no trackers, no data collection. The game does not use the network at all.
• Instant resume — quit mid-room, come back mid-room. Progress survives app closes and restarts.
• Your data stays on your phone — export a full JSON backup or CSV journal any time; restore is previewed before anything changes.
• Designed for one hand — large touch targets, pause anywhere, one-thumb movement.
• Accessible — Dynamic Type on menus and journal, VoiceOver labels, colorblind-safe state glyphs.

A NOTE ON DUAL SCREENS

Delve is built as a standard iPhone app today. The dungeon view and the clue journal are already separate surfaces, designed so that on a future dual-screen iPhone the journal can live permanently on the second display while you explore. No fold is required to play — it's a design target, not a gate.

Delve is original work: no franchise characters, no borrowed assets, no paid shortcuts. Just a tomb, a quest, and your notebook.
